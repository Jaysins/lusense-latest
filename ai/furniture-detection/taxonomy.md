# LuSense Object Detection Taxonomy

## sofa
A sofa or couch intended for seating.

## accent_chair
A standalone decorative/lounge chair that functions as an
accent piece in an interior.

## bed
A bed or primary sleeping furniture structure.

## frames
Decorative wall-mounted artwork, including the distinctive
dartboard-shaped decorative wall pieces used in LuSense's
target interiors.

## pottery
Decorative pots, planters, vases, and similar decorative
ceramic vessels. The plant itself is NOT labeled.

## console
A console table or console-style furniture piece used
against a wall or behind/near seating.
